# Journey: Property Acquisition and Rental for Seekers (UJ02)

This journey defines the comprehensive end-to-end experience of a human property seeker looking to buy or rent a property, navigating from discovery and geospatial evaluation to preference list curation and physical visit scheduling.

---

## Persona

- **Primary Persona:** Property Seeker (prospective buyer or tenant searching for residential or commercial real estate matching lifestyle, budget, spatial amenities, and commuting criteria).
- **Secondary Persona:** Brokerage Web Portal & Assigned Broker Agent (Imobili.ai tenant web interface, Living Map Canvas geospatial engine, and licensed broker managing viewings).

---

## Goal

Search and discover properties using multi-criteria filters or natural language semantic vector search, evaluate neighborhood context and amenity proximities on the interactive Living Map Canvas, inspect rich media (photo gallery and YouTube video tours), create a guest account with social or email login, curate a saved preference list, and seamlessly schedule an in-person viewing with an assigned broker or initiate immediate WhatsApp contact.

---

## Stages

1. **Exploration & Geospatial Discovery Stage:** The seeker filters property listings by city, neighborhood, cardinal regions, price brackets, rooms, suites, typology (house, two-story house, apartment, gated community), or natural language lifestyle prompts. The seeker visualizes candidate properties on the Living Map Canvas with dynamic privacy obfuscation (`obfuscatedHood`) and overlays amenity proximity layers (schools, bakeries, grocery stores, pharmacies, gyms, public transit).
2. **Detail Inspection & Preference Management Stage:** The seeker selects listings from the map or grid to inspect property specifications, high-definition photo galleries, and embedded YouTube video tours. To preserve curated listings, the seeker provisions a guest account using social identity (Google, Facebook) or email and password, dynamically managing a personal preference list.
3. **Broker Engagement & Visit Scheduling Stage:** The seeker initiates a formal visit request for a selected property, receives an interactive booking agenda link showing the assigned broker's calendar slots, selects an appointment time, or immediately opens a direct conversation with the broker via pre-formatted WhatsApp deep linking.
