# OKF Governance Reference and Type Catalog

This document defines the mandatory normative catalog for structuring, naming, classifying, and consuming knowledge across the Imobili.ai repository, compliant with Google's Open Knowledge Format (OKF) standard and the canonical 8-type Atomic Spec Note specification.

---

## Active Types

The ecosystem enforces strictly eight canonical content types:

1. **`vision`**  
   - Prefix: `vis`  
   - Directory: `01-vision/`  
   - Definition: Product vision, positioning, strategic goals, and high-level alignment manifestos.  
   - Required Body Sections: `Purpose`, `Problem`, `Target audience`, `Goals (measurable)`, `Non-goals`, `Success metrics`, `Decomposition map`, `Sequence`.

2. **`concept`**  
   - Prefix: `con`  
   - Directory: `02-concepts/` (`problems/`, `architecture/`, `rules/`)  
   - Definition: Explains what something is, why it exists, and how it works without step-by-step procedural commands. Covers problems, architectural pillars, and operational rules.  
   - Required Body Sections: `Definition`, `Rationale`, `Rules and constraints`, `Examples`, `Related`, `Sequence`.

3. **`runbook`**  
   - Prefix: `run`  
   - Directory: `03-runbooks/`  
   - Definition: Explains how to execute an operational procedure deterministically with preconditions, ordered numbered steps, exact commands, verification, and rollback.  
   - Required Body Sections: `Objective`, `Preconditions`, `Steps` (numbered), `Verification`, `Rollback`, `Sequence`.

4. **`business-requirement`**  
   - Prefix: `req`  
   - Directory: `04-requirements/`  
   - Definition: Cross-cutting business and architectural requirements spanning multiple journeys, tasks, or stories.  
   - Required Body Sections: `Statement`, `Rationale`, `Applies to`, `Measurable acceptance criteria`, `Verification`, `Open questions`, `Sequence`.

5. **`user-journey`**  
   - Prefix: `jrn`  
   - Directory: `User Journeys/`  
   - Definition: End-to-end persona and agent journeys decomposing high-level workflows into sequential tasks.  
   - Required Body Sections: `Persona`, `Goal`, `Stages`, `Tasks`, `Success metric`, `Sequence`.

6. **`user-task`**  
   - Prefix: `tsk`  
   - Directory: `User Journeys/.../User Tasks/`  
   - Definition: Decomposed functional task within a parent user journey grouping executable user stories.  
   - Required Body Sections: `Parent journey`, `Objective`, `Preconditions`, `Stories`, `Sequence`.

7. **`user-story`**  
   - Prefix: `sto`  
   - Directory: `User Journeys/.../User Stories/`  
   - Definition: Atomic implementable story within a task containing testable Given/When/Then acceptance criteria.  
   - Required Body Sections: `Summary`, `Scope in/out`, `Preconditions`, `Expected behavior`, `Acceptance criteria`, `Contracts and data`, `Business rules`, `Edge cases and errors`, `Verification plan`, `Definition of Done`, `Assumptions and open questions`, `Sequence`.

8. **`reference`**  
   - Prefix: `ref`  
   - Directory: `00-meta/`  
   - Definition: Master catalog of types, prefixes, documentation conventions, and repository topology.  
   - Required Body Sections: `Active Types`, `Prefix Table`, `Documentation Conventions`, `Directory Map`, `Sequence`.

## Documentation Conventions

1. **Filename Pattern:** Every regular content note must match `^[a-z]{3}-\d{12}\.md$`. The prefix is exactly 3 lowercase letters mapped to its type, followed by a hyphen and a 12-digit timestamp (`yyyymmddhhmm`).
2. **Identity Equivalence:** The `id` frontmatter field must strictly equal the filename without the `.md` extension (`id == filename`).
3. **Title Rule:** The human-readable title resides in the `title` frontmatter property and never appears in the filename.
4. **Frontmatter Schema:** Frontmatter must strictly contain the 10 fields defined in `./Auxiliary/template-generico.md` in the exact order: `id`, `type`, `title`, `description`, `index`, `tags`, `status`, `generated`, `verified`, `version`.
5. **Timestamp Generation:** Batches of generated notes use strictly increasing timestamps (+1 minute per note) to preserve chronological order and guarantee uniqueness.
6. **Link Syntax (ID Wikilinks):** All note references must use Obsidian-compatible ID wikilinks (syntax: `[[<id>]]`). Relative filesystem paths in note prose are prohibited.
7. **Sequence Section:** Every note forming a flow must include a `## Sequence` section with machine-readable fields: `Position`, `Parent`, `Previous`, `Next`, `Depends on`, and `Applies requirements`.
8. **Concept vs. Runbook Classification:**
   - **Concept:** Answers *what* and *why*. Declarative definitions, models, principles, constraints. No step-by-step commands.
   - **Runbook:** Answers *how*. Procedural steps, preconditions, commands, expected output, verification, and rollback. No abstract theory.
   - Any mixed note must be split into one Concept and one Runbook.
9. **Language and Style:** Notes are written in concise, deterministic English (matching `vis-202609121856.md`). Sentences use imperative verbs and modal "must" for mandatory requirements and "may" for optional ones. Vague words ("fast", "adequate") are prohibited.
10. **Exempt Files:** `index.md` (one per directory) and `log.md` (audit trail in `00-meta/`) must contain NO YAML frontmatter (`---`).
