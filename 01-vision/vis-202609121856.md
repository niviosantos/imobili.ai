# Imobili.ai: The Founding Manifesto and Strategic Vision

*Reclaiming the Real Estate Experience in the Era of Artificial Intelligence*

We stand at a critical threshold where the physical search for a home meets the chaotic limits of a fragmented digital landscape. Traditional real estate systems act as barriers rather than bridges, isolating property owners, buyers, and agencies alike within an analog labyrinth disguised as modern technology. This manifesto establishes the strategic blueprint for a machine-legible, human-centric, and sovereign PropTech ecosystem.

---

## Purpose

The purpose of Imobili.ai is to replace closed, human-only real estate portals with an open, machine-readable, and spatial intelligence platform where autonomous AI agents and human seekers collaborate seamlessly to discover, analyze, and negotiate property transactions without intermediary friction.

---

## Problem

Traditional real estate platforms suffer from systemic structural failures documented in [[con-202609121857]]:
1. **Invisibility of Listings:** Exact property attributes and locations are obscured by brittle keyword search engines.
2. **Human-Centric Blind Spot:** Platforms rely on bloated visual interfaces and CAPTCHAs, preventing autonomous AI agents from discovering and querying inventory.
3. **Static Map Delusion:** Decorative maps display static pins rather than dynamic spatial layers, travel isochrones, and neighborhood amenities.
4. **Cost-Quality Paradox:** Outdated legacy software vendors compete on race-to-the-bottom pricing, accumulating severe technical debt.
5. **High-Friction Licensing and Silos:** Proprietary vendor lock-in prevents independent brokers and boutique agencies from running sovereign infrastructure.

---

## Target audience

1. **Autonomous AI Agents & Copilots:** Intelligent browsers and autonomous agentic bots searching and transacting real estate programmatically on behalf of clients.
2. **Property Seekers (Buyers & Tenants):** Individuals looking for homes matching lifestyle, lighting, commute, and neighborhood preferences.
3. **Real Estate Agencies & Independent Brokers:** Boutique brokerages and agencies requiring sovereign, self-service, multi-tenant digital infrastructure.
4. **Property Owners:** Landlords and sellers seeking maximum visibility across agentic search networks.

---

## Goals (measurable)

1. **Machine Interoperability:** Expose 100% of public tenant inventory via standardized machine-readable Agent-to-Agent (A2A) interfaces ([[con-202609121902]]) and Web-native Model Context Protocol (WebMCP) endpoints ([[con-202609121905]], [[con-202609121907]]) for autonomous AI agent, copilot, and browser interaction.
2. **Intent-Based Retrieval:** Achieve sub-second semantic retrieval using dense vector embeddings for natural language queries with dynamic irrelevance floor filtering ([[con-202609121901]]).
3. **Geospatial Reasoning Surface:** Provide an active Living Map Canvas supporting real-time transit isochrones, amenity polygon generation, and configurable neighborhood obfuscation ([[con-202609121903]]).
4. **Zero Vendor Lock-in & Strict Isolation:** Deliver a 100% open-source, multi-tenant architecture with strict data isolation (no shared pools) enabling autonomous onboarding in under 5 minutes ([[con-202609121904]], [[run-202609121908]]).
5. **Context Economy:** Ensure all platform documentation and specifications operate under pure Markdown-native standards ([[con-202609121906]]).

---

## Non-goals

1. Building a centralized, monopolistic property portal that monetizes by charging predatory listing fees.
2. Creating shared or federated listing pools that compromise tenant vector isolation or leak private brokerage inventory.
3. Supporting unverified or anonymous contract offers; all transaction offers strictly require prior account registration and internal brokerage workflow validation.
4. Supporting opaque binary document formats or unverified visual web-scraping interfaces.

---

## Success metrics

1. **A2A & WebMCP Interoperability Rate:** 100% deterministic JSON-RPC and MCP tool response rate on valid agent queries.
2. **Onboarding Duration:** Tenant provisioning completed in under 300 seconds using standard runbooks.
3. **Semantic Query Accuracy:** Greater than 90% top-5 relevance ranking on natural language intent queries.
4. **Standards Compliance:** 100% compliance with Google OKF specifications and canonical 8-type Atomic Spec Note standards across all repository notes.
