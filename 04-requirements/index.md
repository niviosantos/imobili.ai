# Index: 04-requirements

This directory stores cross-cutting business requirements that apply across multiple journeys, tasks, and stories in the Imobili.ai platform.

---

## Mapped Business Requirements

- [[req-202609121910]] - Multi-Tenant Data and Index Isolation - Mandates strict cryptographic segregation, isolated vector collections, and prohibition of shared multi-brokerage listing pools.
- [[req-202609121911]] - Cryptographic Audit Trail and Transaction Integrity - Mandates registered user accounts, internal brokerage workflow validation, cryptographic signing, and immutable audit logs for all transactions.
- [[req-202609121912]] - A2A Rate Limiting and Service Protection - Mandates two-tier rate limits (Public/Anonymous and Registered Agent) and anti-scraping safeguards.
- [[req-202609121913]] - Data Privacy and LGPD Compliance - Mandates personal data masking, tenant-configurable neighborhood obfuscation (obfuscatedHood), and LGPD compliance.
